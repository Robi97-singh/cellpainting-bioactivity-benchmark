# Archive: first pilot

An early pilot on 16 targets (`pilot_16_targets/`) and the ResNet-50 Grad-CAM overlays made for it (`resnet50_gradcam/`).
These targets were the ResNet's most confident cases and half of them were noise images, so the pilot overstated the
explanation methods. They are kept for the record only. The reported explainability results are in `../random_65_targets/`,
`../xai_vit/` and `../xai_cnn/`.
